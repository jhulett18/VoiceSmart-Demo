BrightSmile Dental Clinic - Standard Operating Procedures
(SOP)
Title: BrightSmile Dental Clinic - SOP Version: 1.0 | Date: August 2025 Scope: This SOP applies to all patient
interactions, clinical services, and administrative tasks. 1. Patient Intake - Greet patient professionally and
confirm appointment time. - Verify insurance coverage and collect identification. - Update patient record in the
dental management system. 2. Appointment Procedures - Confirm procedure details with patient before treatment.
- Follow sterilization protocol for all instruments. - Document all services rendered in patient file. 3. Cancellation
Policy - Require 24-hour notice for cancellations. - Apply $50 fee for late cancellations unless due to emergency.
4. Emergency Handling - Provide immediate triage for walk-in emergencies. - Notify attending dentist and
prepare treatment room within 5 minutes.
BrightSmile Dental Clinic - Service Level Agreement (SLA)
Title: BrightSmile Dental Clinic - SLA Version: 1.0 | Date: August 2025 Scope: Service commitments to all
patients. 1. Response Times - Phone calls answered within 3 rings during business hours. - Email inquiries
responded to within 1 business day. 2. Appointment Wait Time - Patients seen within 15 minutes of scheduled
time. 3. Quality Standards - 100% compliance with sterilization protocols. - Treatment plans reviewed by a
licensed dentist before execution. 4. Service Availability - Business hours: Mon–Fri 9:00–18:00 - Emergency
coverage during business hours.
ProFix Auto Repair - Standard Operating Procedures (SOP)
Title: ProFix Auto Repair - SOP Version: 1.0 | Date: August 2025 Scope: Applies to all vehicle intake, repair, and
customer service processes. 1. Vehicle Check-In - Record VIN, mileage, and customer concerns. - Photograph
vehicle condition upon arrival. 2. Diagnostic Process - Use OBD-II scan for electronic diagnostics. - Conduct
physical inspection per checklist. 3. Repair Procedures - Follow manufacturer specifications for all repairs. - Test
drive post-repair to verify issue resolution. 4. Warranty Policy - Provide 12-month/12,000-mile warranty on parts
and labor.
ProFix Auto Repair - Service Level Agreement (SLA)
Title: ProFix Auto Repair - SLA Version: 1.0 | Date: August 2025 Scope: Service commitments to all customers.
1. Service Times - Oil changes completed within 45 minutes of appointment. - Brake pad replacements completed
same day. 2. Customer Communication - Provide written estimates before repairs. - Notify customers
immediately of any scope changes. 3. Quality Standards - Use OEM or equivalent parts. - Road test every vehicle
before release. 4. Service Availability - Business hours: Mon–Sat 8:00–18:00
PulsePoint Fitness Gym - Standard Operating Procedures
(SOP)
Title: PulsePoint Fitness Gym - SOP Version: 1.0 | Date: August 2025 Scope: Applies to all membership
management, class scheduling, and trainer assignments. 1. Member Onboarding - Provide facility tour and review
membership terms. - Collect health waiver and emergency contact info. 2. Class Scheduling - Members must
book classes via app or reception. - Maintain maximum class size for safety. 3. Trainer Assignments - Match
personal trainers to client goals and availability. - Review client progress monthly. 4. Safety Protocols - Inspect
all equipment daily before opening. - Report and tag out faulty equipment immediately.
PulsePoint Fitness Gym - Service Level Agreement (SLA)
Title: PulsePoint Fitness Gym - SLA Version: 1.0 | Date: August 2025 Scope: Service commitments to all
members. 1. Class Availability - Guarantee at least 3 available classes per day in each category. 2. Trainer
Response - Trainers respond to member inquiries within 1 business day. 3. Equipment Standards - 100% of
equipment inspected daily. - Repairs initiated within 24 hours of fault detection. 4. Facility Hours - Open
Mon–Sun 6:00–22:00
